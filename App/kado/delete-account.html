Kado — Account & Data Deletion

In the app (instant): open Kado → Settings → My Account → Danger Zone → Delete Account & Server Data. This permanently removes your account, profile, daily nutrition summaries, and AI usage counters from our servers.

By request: email support.kado@balshoo.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete your account and associated server data within 30 days.

What stays: your detailed food logs are stored only on your own device — never on our servers — and are removed when you uninstall the app. Community contributions (product submissions) become anonymous once your account is deleted.
